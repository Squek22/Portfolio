About Me

My name is Chris, I'm an Army Retiree turned into Cyber Defender. I've always had a passion for working with computers and tinkering with them to figure out how they work, however only recently have I decided to work more indepth with them. I'm in the final year of my undergraduate degree in Cybersecurity, and work full time as the Director of Information Security for a Managed Service Provider in Utah. I love what I do, since retiring from the Army there's been a void in the sense of protecting others, now I am able to offer that protection to those who need it most.

Small to Medium Size businesses often times can not afford to protect their digital assets the way they should be, so as a MSP we are able to come in and not only run their Information Technology program for them, but offer Information Security or Cybersecurity to them as well. Providing the knowledge and tools to them, while they can focus on their speciality, whether it's finance, medical or construction. Using this passion, I've started my own company (CMISecurity L.L.C) to begin offering Cybersecurity Consulting on a personal level, the more people who are aware of what is going on in the world when it comes to cyber threats, the safer we can all be.

When I'm not working in a computer, I'm spending time with my family, fishing, or playing video games. I often find myself spending free time tinkering with new cyber tech though, or trying to figure out current cyber tech works. It's a never ending passion, and certainly an industry that won't be slowing down anytime soon.
